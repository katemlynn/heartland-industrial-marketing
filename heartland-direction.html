Frequently asked questions

What's the best marketing strategy for a metals company? +

It depends on what you sell, who you sell to, and the jobs you want to win more of. For most metals manufacturers, it starts with a clearly defined ideal customer and a website built to convert them — then a Google Business Profile, paid search, signage, and a steady review pipeline.

How long does it take to start generating leads? +

Paid search and a tightened Google Business Profile can produce inbound quote requests within a few weeks. SEO and reputation-building compound over three to six months. We sequence the fast wins first so you have momentum while the slower channels build.

Should I invest in SEO or paid ads first? +

Both, in that order, but start paid first. Paid search puts you in front of buyers looking today. SEO takes three to six months to compound but is the cheapest long-term channel — under-investing in either usually costs more in the end.

How much should a metals company spend on marketing? +

Most owner-operated metals manufacturers should spend three to seven percent of revenue on marketing — lower with strong word of mouth, higher when growing into new geographies or product lines. We'll tell you what makes sense for your situation on the call.

Most of my sales go through contractors — how do I market around that? +

One of the most common patterns we see: you sell the materials, GCs install, end owners never learn your name. We use post-install review systems, on-site QR codes routing back to you, and contractor partnership programs that share credit.
